PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This page explains the current privacy-related behavior of the Manifest Esports website. Please review and update it before launch so it accurately reflects your operator details, jurisdiction, and any services you enable.

Information you provide

The current website may let you enter information into front-end demo forms. The included demo login/signup stores a name and email in your browser's local storage only; it does not create a secure account or send that information to a server. Do not enter passwords or sensitive information into these demo forms.

Browser storage

The demo interface uses local storage to remember its simulated signed-in state on the same browser. You can clear this data through your browser's site-data settings. This is not server-side authentication.

Third-party services

The site currently references Google Fonts and links to third-party services such as Discord, YouTube, and Instagram. Those services may receive technical information when you visit or follow their links, according to their own policies. Confirm which third-party resources are used before launch.

Analytics and cookies

This policy does not claim that analytics or advertising tools are installed. If you add analytics, advertising, embedded media, or non-essential cookies, update this policy and implement consent controls where required by applicable law.

Security and retention

Because this site is currently static, do not submit confidential data through it. If server-side forms, accounts, or databases are added, document what is collected, why it is collected, who receives it, and how long it is retained.

Children and contact

Do not knowingly collect personal information from children without meeting applicable legal requirements. Before launch, replace this paragraph with a monitored contact email or postal address for privacy requests; no contact address has been supplied for this draft.

Changes

This policy should be revised whenever the site's data practices change. This starter page is informational and is not legal advice.
